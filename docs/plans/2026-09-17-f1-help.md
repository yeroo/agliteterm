# F1 Help overlay (agwinterm parity)

agwinterm has had an F1 help card since 0.14 (Help.cs): how the app works, the effective key
bindings, Esc to close. agliteterm had none: F1 went to the shell and the only orientation was
the About box. An outside contributor sketched a backport (agliteterm #98, withdrawn to their
fork); this is the in-tree version.

## What it does

- `F1` (plain, in the terminal) opens a card over the terminal area: GETTING STARTED, FOCUS AND
  NAVIGATION, KEY BINDINGS (the registry bindings File ▸ Keyboard… shows, bound ones only,
  sorted by label), CUSTOM COMMANDS (keymap.conf's bindings and the leader chord, when any) and
  MORE. The title carries the version. Help ▸ Help and the palette row "Help" open the same card
  (one WM_COMMAND, `IDM_HELP`).
- While open the card owns the keyboard: Esc and F1 close, Up/Down/PgUp/PgDn/Home/End and the
  wheel scroll, everything else is swallowed (the WM_CHAR of a swallowed key included, through
  the existing `g_swallowChar`). A click outside the card closes it; a right click does nothing.
- `window state` reports `helpVisible` (beyond the contract; extra fields are allowed), which is
  what the UI suite reads.

## Decisions

- **The text is host-free.** `src/help.h` (`help::lines`, `help::isSection`, `help::clampScroll`)
  builds the card from what the host passes in and is unit-tested by `test/help.unit.cpp` in the
  wave3 pattern; main.cpp only gathers (`helpRebuild`), owns the state and paints.
- **Opening help closes the palette**, never the reverse: the two would otherwise stack, and Esc
  would have to know which to close first.
- **Plain F1 wins over the shell**, as in agwinterm, so a full-screen program loses its F1 to the
  terminal. A custom command bound to bare `f1` in keymap.conf is shadowed the same way (the
  Keyboard dialog cannot bind a bare key at all: its rules add Ctrl).
- **F1 in the sidebar does not open help.** The sidebar is a native TreeView child and takes the
  key itself; help is the terminal's F1, as the card says. Not worth a subclass.
- **The card covers the terminal area only** (sidebar, toolbar and status bar stay), like the
  palette, so a status cue landing on a sidebar row is never hidden. No dimming: GDI has no cheap
  alpha and lite's overlays do not dim.
- Headings are pure capitals so `isSection` needs no parenthetical exception (agwinterm's own
  "KEY BINDINGS (effective — keymap.conf applied)" fails its own rule and paints as body text).
- **Popup terminals keep their keyboard** (revmux r1 Major). `popupProc` shares `handleKeyDown`;
  it now says it is a popup, and the help branches are the frame's only: F1 in the quick, scratch
  or overlay terminal reaches its program, and a card open on the frame never swallows a popup's
  keys. Showing a popup closes the card, as it closes the dashboard.
- **Nothing opens under the card** (revmux r1 Major). Any menu or toolbar command other than Help
  itself closes the card before it runs (`OnCommand`), `togglePalette` closes it when opening, the
  `dashboard` verb and `pick.open` clear it. Alt chords pass through `helpKey` to DefWindowProc,
  so Alt+F4 and the menu bar work with the card up.
- **The card lists effective bindings only**: a built-in whose chord a keymap.conf line took is
  left out (custom bindings run first), of two lines on one chord only the last (the one that
  runs), and nothing on a bare F1 (help's key; a leader on bare F1 takes its sequences with it).
  A leader sequence still inside its two-second window takes F1 first, so `map leader f1` works.
  Opening help drops a pending leader. Binding rows longer than the card end in "...".
- The card does not claim F10 opens the menu bar (lite sends F10 to the shell) or that
  Ctrl+Shift+V pastes (Paste is a Keyboard binding with no default).

## Review

- revmux r1 (claude-only, four agents on `20c9394`): two Majors (popups, stacking) and four Minors
  (the 74-column promise for custom rows, the leader window, shadowed rows on the card, Alt+F4,
  the F10 / Ctrl+Shift+V lines) - all folded.

## Verification

- `test/help.unit.ps1` (registered in run-all): text, ordering, sections, scroll clamps.
- `test/wave3-ui-cases.ps1` (selection-ui, CI): F1 opens, a letter and Esc are swallowed and the
  pane text is unchanged, F1 toggles, the menu command opens, a click outside closes.
- Native build; a sandbox screenshot in the PR.
